Current law

Four rules that shape the plan

Federal exclusion, not a property exemption

The 2026 federal basic exclusion is $15,000,000 per decedent. Real estate alone is not the taxable estate: other assets, adjusted taxable gifts, deductions, debts and any DSUE amount must be included.

California has no current estate-tax return

For deaths on or after January 1, 2005, California does not require a state estate-tax return. Federal filing and income-tax obligations can still apply.

Occupancy preserves less than many expect

For a high-value inherited primary residence, the simplified Proposition 19 formula is market value − applicable exclusion when the former assessed value plus exclusion is below market value. Renting does not qualify.

Three clocks can start at death

Trust notice may be due within 60 days; assessor death reporting is generally due within 150 days; a required federal estate return and payment are generally due within 9 months. Confirm applicability with professionals.
