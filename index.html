Executive summary

Do not choose a path until the private facts are verified.

This public guide uses synthetic inputs only. Property A is a generic example of a possible trust inheritance; Property B is a generic example of an already-owned rental property. No household value, rent, bill, lease, location identifier or insurance record is loaded into the published model.

What would change the answer? Trust authority and title, date-of-death value and tax basis, estate liquidity and prior gifts, eligibility and occupancy under Proposition 19, actual market rent, property-tax bills, insurance quotes, repair needs, transaction costs, tax treatment and the household’s value of living in the home.

How to use the comparisons. The tables demonstrate how assumptions change pure and mixed paths. Their rankings are not recommendations. Keep each property’s ownership and tax treatment separate, use the private checklist to obtain verified inputs, and rerun the analysis locally before acting.

03 / example finding

What the synthetic example demonstrates

The example model compares the same transfer date and horizon across live, rent, sell, phased-investing and mixed paths. Its output is conditional on invented property values, costs, returns, tax simplifications and stress shapes. It cannot rank a real household’s choices. A real comparison needs private appraisals, tax basis, current bills, insurance and rent evidence, trust terms, occupancy preferences and adviser review.

03b / reinvestment

Reinvestment risk, beside the headline

Proceeds that wait before they are invested earn the parking yield, and their value at the end depends on that yield. The observed U.S. Treasury par yields on 13 Nov 2026 were 4.32% for 3 months and 4.88% for 2 years. The 2.50% row is a stress assumption for falling short rates, not an observation. Only the parking yield changes between rows. Values are per $100,000 of proceeds, after 10 years, with post-trough growth between 4% and 8% a year.

Agree the conditions before the figures arrive. The pre-commitment register lists, for living, renting, selling and every mixed path, the condition the household writes down and dates first, the falsifier that would void it, and the official rule it rests on. Every threshold on that page is blank on purpose: a public site cannot choose for you, and choosing after seeing one projection is how a decision gets reverse-engineered.
