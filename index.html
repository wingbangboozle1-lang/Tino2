Executive summary

Should we keep, rent, or sell the inherited Cupertino house?

This site collects, organizes, and analyzes the requirements for deciding what to do with two homes — a Cupertino house (ZIP 95014) to be inherited through a trust around 2040–2050, and a San Francisco home (ZIP 94122) that is already a primary residence with two rented units. Bottom-line recommendation

Sell the Cupertino house shortly after inheritance (modeled year 2045), reinvest the proceeds, and keep the San Francisco home as primary residence plus rental.

Why: Even with Proposition 19 relief, occupying the Cupertino home costs roughly $61,440/yr in property tax alone at 2045 projected values; renting it out triggers full reassessment (≈ $74,500/yr tax) and nets only ≈ $26,120/yr. Selling is nearly tax-free (stepped-up basis at death), and the ≈ $5,662,110 net proceeds invested at a 6% planning assumption throw off ≈ $339,730 in year one — several times the net rental cash flow, with no tax, insurance, or maintenance burden. The SF property (≈ $150,480/yr gross rent against a low assessed-value tax bill) is a keeper.
