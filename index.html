Working conclusion (modeled — revisit at inheritance with verified figures)

  • Keep the San Francisco house as a rental. Very low Proposition 13 property tax (assessed value rises max 2%/yr with no ownership change) and strong modeled positive cash flow (≈ $77,100/yr before maintenance). Owner may live there as primary residence and still collect rent.
  • Sell the Cupertino house at inheritance unless an heir needs a primary residence there. Step-up in basis (IRC §1014) minimizes capital gains tax on sale. Keeping it triggers a Proposition 19 reassessment with modeled annual property tax of ≈ $57,090 (live-in, partial exclusion) or ≈ $70,670 (rent out, full reassessment). Break-even rent ≈ $5,890/mo before other costs.
  • If an heir will live in the Cupertino home: move in within 1 year of transfer and file BOE-19-P plus the homeowner's exemption to preserve the partial exclusion.
  • Federal estate tax is likely $0 if the total estate (houses + [INHERITANCE_ASSETS]) stays at or below $15,000,000 per person ($30,000,000 married couple with portability). Amounts above are taxed at 40% and paid by the estate.
Projection warning. All forward figures (home value growth, tax rates, insurance, maintenance, break-even rent) rely on assumptions and unverified inputs. See the Flagged for Review page and model documentation. Verify against county assessor records and obtain binding insurance quotes before acting.

Recommended next steps (no personal information required)

  • Confirm both properties are titled in the trust ([TRUSTEE] to verify with the trust document).
  • Verify owner-provided tax/assessment records against the Santa Clara County Assessor and San Francisco Assessor-Recorder.
  • Obtain binding homeowners / landlord insurance quotes for ZIP 95014 and 94122.
  • If the estate may exceed $15,000,000 per person, consult a CA estate attorney and CPA well before the inheritance date.
  • At inheritance: obtain a date-of-death appraisal (stepped-up basis) and, if keeping the Cupertino home as a primary residence, file BOE-19-P within 3 years and the homeowner's exemption within 1 year.
