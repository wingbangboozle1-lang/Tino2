Key Findings

  1. No California estate or inheritance tax. California imposes neither a state estate tax nor an inheritance tax; only the federal estate tax can apply, and only above the exemption. [CA FTB] [IRS estate tax FAQ]
  2. Federal exemption is $15,000,000 per person for 2026 ($30,000,000 married with portability), made permanent and inflation-indexed from 2027 by the Working Families Tax Cuts / One Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-21); top rate 40% above the exemption. [IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32] [IRS newsroom]
  3. Step-up in basis (IRC §1014) resets inherited property to date-of-death fair market value — selling the inherited Cupertino house shortly after inheriting typically triggers ~$0 capital gains tax. [IRS Pub. 551]
  4. Prop 19 is the big property-tax trap. The parent–child exclusion applies only if the Heir moves into the inherited home as a primary residence within 1 year and files BOE-19-P (within 3 years) plus the homeowners’ exemption (within 1 year); the protected amount is capped at factored base year value + $1,044,586 (2025–2027 adjustment). Rental/other property gets no exclusion — full reassessment to market value. [BOE Prop 19] [BOE Prop 19 Fact Sheet]
  5. Projection at 4%/yr appreciation: Property A market value ≈ $5,370,000 by 2040 and ≈ $6,530,000 by 2045; assessed value (Prop 13, 2%/yr) ≈ $726,420 by 2040 and ≈ $802,030 by 2045.
  6. Recommendation (see Decision Analysis): at inheritance, sell Property A and reinvest (step-up basis makes the sale nearly tax-free; at ~2045 full reassessment would push property tax to ≈ $86,870/yr, which would consume most of the potential rental income). Keep Property B as owner-occupied rental (≈ $71,680/yr net operating income, proj.). If the Heir must live in Property A, move in within 1 year to claim the partial Prop 19 exclusion — worth ≈ $13,890/yr in tax savings vs. renting it out (2045 projection).
  7. Insurance: California average homeowners premium ≈ $1,571/yr (CDI/NAIC data, Feb 2026); landlord/dwelling-fire policies for CA rentals average ≈ $1,700/yr (NAIC 2023 data). [CA Dept. of Insurance]
  8. Utilities: Cupertino water (San Jose Water Co., CPUC-approved tiers) averages ≈ $140/mo for ~10 Ccf; solar under NEM 3.0 typically yields a small monthly credit (net ≈ −$10 to −$20/mo per user data). SF water/sewer for a multi-unit building ≈ $330–$400/mo per user data (SFPUC rates rising ~10%/yr). [SJWC] [SFPUC] [CPUC]
