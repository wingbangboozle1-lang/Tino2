# Research record

Official sources were reviewed on **2026-11-10**. The public source register is `site/data/sources.js`; `site/sources.html` renders it with the legal-change watchlist and assumption register.

## Evidence boundaries

- Official government pages, statutes, forms and rate publications support current law and published benchmarks.
- User-supplied values, bills, rents, insurance figures and utility ranges are household planning inputs, not independently verified official observations.
- Derived effective property-tax ratios are arithmetic from household inputs, not official tax-rate-area rates.
- Future market values, rents, expenses, investment returns, tax rates, Proposition 19 exclusions and federal estate exclusions are scenarios, not forecasts.
- Unknown values remain blank and are listed in `site/review.html`.
- Pending legislation is never treated as enacted. California SB 974 is chaptered but narrow; it is not modeled as a broad Proposition 19 rollback.

## Material current-law anchors

- 2026 federal basic estate exclusion: **$15,000,000** per decedent; future amounts are indexed under current law but not yet published.
- California estate-tax return: none for deaths on or after 2005-01-01.
- Proposition 19 intergenerational exclusion: **$1,044,586** for transfers from 2025-02-16 through 2027-02-15; primary-residence and claim requirements apply.
- BOE-502-D: generally within 150 days after death for trust/other nonprobate transfers, for each parcel in each county.
- California trust notice: generally within 60 days of listed events under Probate Code §16061.7.
- Form 706 and payment: generally nine months after death; filing extension does not automatically extend payment.
- FY2026 HUD Small Area FMRs are gross-rent benchmarks, not guaranteed contract rents.
- SF covered-unit allowable increase: 1.6% for 2026-03-01 through 2027-02-28. Future extrapolation is an assumption.
- SF Rent Board fee: $59 per dwelling unit for 2026–27 unless exempt; unit exemptions and inventory/license compliance require verification.
- SF FY2026–27 combined ad valorem property-tax rate: 1.18162182%; this does not reconcile a parcel’s total bill or supplemental charges.

## Privacy

Research records must not contain private names, street addresses, parcel numbers, account identifiers, trust instruments, tax returns, tenant records or other personal documents. Only generalized household scenario inputs and official public sources belong in the repository.
