The 7% return on equities has no official counterpart: none of the four bodies above projects a stock-market return. It stays an assumption, as the Sources page already says.

**Reading.** The base case sits close to the official long-run projections on home prices, consumer prices and the safe rate. Two things do not: insurance has risen three times as fast as the model assumes, and 2026 inflation is running a full point above what the Congressional Budget Office projected in February. Neither changes the best plan. The stress pages already cover a spell of high inflation.

**What closed.** The site carried a flag that the 2.3% and 4.4% figures came from secondary summaries. Both were read in the Office's own table.
