Inheritance steps

What to do, in order, when a house held in a California trust passes to the next generation. Deadlines are counted from the date of death. People appear as [OWNER] (the parent), [TRUSTEE] (whoever takes over the trust) and [HEIR].

Deadlines at a glance

  1. Day 0Date of death: appraisal date, new tax basis, change of ownership
  2. 60 daysTrust notification to beneficiaries and heirs
  3. 150 daysChange in ownership statement to each county assessor
  4. 9 monthsFederal estate tax return, if required or to elect portability
  5. 1 yearHeir moved in and homeowners’ exemption claimed — only if living in Property A
  6. 3 yearsExclusion claim filed — only if living in Property A
