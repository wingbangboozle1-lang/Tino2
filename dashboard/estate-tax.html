How much room is left before any estate tax

Room = exclusion − market value of the home(s). Whatever cash and investments the estate also holds must fit in that room; each $1,000,000 above it would cost about $400,000 in federal tax. "Two exclusions" applies when a spouse died earlier and the unused exclusion was claimed on a timely return.

Reading the result

  • The two homes alone stay under one person's exclusion in every scenario shown, so the homes by themselves do not trigger federal estate tax.
  • In the base scenario for 2045, other assets above about $13,390,660 (one exclusion) would be taxed at the top rate.
  • Home values in the base and high scenarios grow faster than the exclusion is projected to, so the room shrinks over time as a share of the estate.
  • Ways people stay under the line, all for an estate attorney to assess: annual gifts within the per-recipient amount, filing a return at the first spouse's death to preserve the unused exclusion, and charitable bequests.
