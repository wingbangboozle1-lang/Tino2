Property A: the tax bill after inheritance

The low assessed value does not pass to the Heir intact. Because the house is worth far more than the old assessed value plus the exclusion amount, moving in only takes about $1,401,810 off the taxable value in 2040; not moving in means tax on the full market value. Worked example at today's values: $3,100,000 − $1,044,586 = $2,055,410 taxable if the Heir moves in, against $550,540 now.
