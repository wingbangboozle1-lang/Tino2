Property-tax rules

QuestionCurrent rule / limitationReference
What changes at transfer?Change in ownership can cause reassessment. The Proposition 13 factored base year value generally grows by the inflation factor, capped at 2%, absent reassessment or new construction; declining-market relief can apply.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
When can Proposition 19 help?Generally an eligible parent-child family-home transfer requires the transferor’s principal residence and the transferee to make it their principal residence within one year and continue occupancy. Special rules apply to other eligible transfers.California BOE · fact sheet ↗
How much value is protected?For 16 Feb 2025–15 Feb 2027 the additional allowance is approximately $1,044,590, rounded from the official amount. If fair market value exceeds factored base value plus the allowance, the excess is added to the factored value. The Board states the allowance is adjusted every other February 16; a county summary page describes a different cadence, so the Board’s Letters to Assessors govern. Later amounts are unknown.BOE · 2025/009 ↗ · Santa Clara County Assessor ↗
How the allowance is carried forward hereLetter To Assessors 2025/009, read 15 Nov 2026, quotes article XIII A §2.1(c)(4): the amount is adjusted every other 16 February, beginning 2023, to reflect the percentage change in the House Price Index for California for the prior calendar year as determined by the Federal Housing Finance Agency. The letter states the FHFA 2024 California change as 2.15%, which is why the published amount is $1,044,586. The guide now carries the allowance forward biennially on that method, using 2.15% as the forward basis; every carried-forward year is a projection, because future FHFA changes are not published yet.BOE · 2025/009 ↗ · FHFA California house price index via FRED · CASTHPI ↗
What the allowance would be, carried forwardAt 2.15% a biennial step the allowance would be about $1,067,050 for a 2027 transfer and about $1,292,200 for a 2045 transfer. These are arithmetic on a published method, not published figures: the Board publishes the actual figure for each two-year window, and a different house-price path changes them.BOE · 2025/009 ↗ · FHFA California house price index via FRED · CASTHPI ↗
Does renting qualify?Renting the inherited home instead of making it the eligible transferee’s principal residence generally fails the family-home condition. Confirm facts with the assessor.California BOE · Prop 19 ↗

Estate, gift and income tax

ItemOfficial current informationCase-specific amountReference
Federal basic exclusion$15,000,000 for deaths in 2026; $15,480,000 in 2027. Prior taxable gifts and an available transferred spousal exclusion may change the available amount.— unverifiedIRS · 2027 adjustments ↗
Filing threshold versus taxable estateThe filing threshold generally tests the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts and certain exemption amounts. Deductions are relevant to taxable estate and tax, not a simple subtraction from the gross filing test.— unverifiedIRS · estate tax ↗
CaliforniaThe Controller says no California estate-tax return is required for deaths on or after 1 January 2005. Other taxes, and taxes of other jurisdictions if relevant, are separate.— unverifiedCA Controller · estate tax ↗
Income-tax basisInherited property generally receives date-of-death FMV basis, subject to exceptions and elections. One particular irrevocable grantor-trust fact pattern outside the estate did not receive an adjustment.— unverifiedIRS · Rev. Rul. 2023-2 ↗
Retirement accountsTaxable inherited IRA distributions may be income to the beneficiary; treatment and timing depend on account and beneficiary rules.— unverifiedIRS · retirement beneficiaries ↗
Federal home-sale exclusionUp to $250,000, or $500,000 for qualifying joint filers, subject to ownership, use and other conditions; it is not automatic. IRS Topic 701, read 15 Nov 2026, states the ownership and use tests: owned for at least 24 months of the 5 years before the sale, and used as a residence for at least 24 months of the previous 5 years, both met inside the 5-year period ending on the sale date.— unverified; the guide now applies it to the living-then-sell paths, because those paths occupy the home for at least 24 months before sellingIRS · sale of home ↗
California treatment of a capital gainThe Franchise Tax Board states plainly: “California does not have a lower rate for capital gains. All capital gains are taxed as ordinary income.” The 2025 Schedule CA (540) instructions repeat it: no special rate for long-term capital gain exists.— the model therefore applies California ordinary rates to the gain, not a reduced federal-style rateFTB · capital gains and losses ↗ · FTB · 2025 Schedule CA (540) instructions ↗
California 1% tax above one million dollarsThe 2025 Form 540 instructions, line 62, read 15 Nov 2026, call this the Behavioral Health Services Tax: if taxable income is more than $1,000,000, subtract $1,000,000 and multiply the remainder by 1%. The instructions use that name; older material calls it the mental health services tax.— applies to taxable income over $1,000,000, so the model treats it as a marginal addition to the top 12.3% rateFTB · 2025 Form 540 instructions ↗
Combined rate used for a large gain20% federal top rate + 3.8% net investment income tax + 12.3% California top rate + 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax = 37.1% at the margin. Lower California brackets make the effective figure lower for most filers: the range this site tests is 23.8% to 37.1%.— a top marginal rate, not an average; the household’s own bracket is not published hereIRS · Topic 409 capital gains ↗ · IRS · Topic 559 net investment income tax ↗ · FTB · 2025 Form 540 rate schedules (PDF) ↗ · FTB · 2025 Form 540 instructions ↗

Transfer and sale taxes the county states

ItemWhat the official page statesCase-specific amountReference
Documentary transfer taxThe County Clerk-Recorder states the tax is due on all changes of ownership unless an applicable statutory exemption is cited, computed at 55 cents for each $500 or fractional portion of real property value, excluding liens or encumbrances already of record. On a larger base that is $110 for each $100,000 of consideration, or 0.11%.— not assumed to apply to a transfer at death; see the exemption row belowSanta Clara County Clerk-Recorder · Recording real estate ↗ · CA Revenue & Taxation Code §11911 ↗
Exemption for a transfer by reason of deathRevenue and Taxation Code §11930, read 15 Nov 2026, states that the tax shall not apply to any deed, instrument or other writing that transfers realty “by reason of the death of any person”, outright or in trust for the benefit of any person or entity. The county page still requires the statutory exemption to be cited on the document.— an exemption the statute appears to give; counsel or title should cite §11930 on the instrument rather than rely on this pageCA Revenue & Taxation Code §11930 ↗ · Santa Clara County Clerk-Recorder · Recording real estate ↗
What that means for the two datesRead together: the inheritance transfer itself is the one the statute exempts, and a later sale by the heir is not. The model therefore charges the 0.11% county rate on a later sale only, and charges nothing at the inheritance transfer.— modelled as a sale cost of 0.11% of the price, alongside negotiated sale costsCA Revenue & Taxation Code §11930 ↗ · Santa Clara County Clerk-Recorder · Recording real estate ↗
City add-onThe same county page states that properties located within San Jose, Palo Alto and Mountain View are taxed at triple that rate — $330 for each $100,000 of consideration, or 0.33% — citing R&T Code §11911. Cupertino is not named on that page, and a city add-on is set by ordinance.— unverifiedSanta Clara County Clerk-Recorder · Recording real estate ↗ · CA Revenue & Taxation Code §11911 ↗
Preliminary Change of Ownership ReportThe county states an additional $20 is due if a PCOR is required but is not completed, signed and submitted at recording.— a filing question for the trustee or transfereeSanta Clara County Clerk-Recorder · Recording real estate ↗ · California RTC §480 ↗
How the tax is computed on the documentThe recorded document must indicate whether the tax is computed on the consideration or on the consideration less liens remaining at the time of sale, and the declaration must be signed by the party computing the tax or claiming the exemption.— proceduralSanta Clara County Clerk-Recorder · Recording real estate ↗
San Francisco transfer taxSan Francisco administers its own secured property-tax program through the Treasurer. The city transfer-tax rate schedule was not re-read from an official page in this review, so no amount is stated here.— unverifiedSF Treasurer · secured property tax ↗
