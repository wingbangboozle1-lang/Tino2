Every tax that touches this decision.

Property tax, income tax and inheritance thresholds, kept separate, with the exact amount in force and the date it was checked.

Anonymous by designOfficial sources linkedProjections are estimates
Four different taxes are involved and they are constantly confused. Property tax is an annual local tax on assessed value. Income tax applies when the house is rented or sold. The federal estate tax applies to the decedent’s estate, not to the heir. California has no inheritance tax. Each has its own table below.
01 / property tax

Annual property tax

QuestionCurrent ruleSource
How is the bill calculated?One per cent of assessed value plus voter-approved bond rates for the tax rate area, which differ parcel by parcel.Santa Clara County · FY2025-26 tax rate book ↗
How fast can the assessed value rise?Each year by the California CPI change, capped at 2%. In 2026-27 the factor was 1.02 because CPI rose 3.307%. Over 51 years the full 2% applied in 40 years and the average was 1.81%.BOE LTA 2026/002 · inflation factor ↗
What happens on inheritance?A change in ownership normally reassesses to market value, which is where the sevenfold jump in the rental case comes from.California BOE · Proposition 19 ↗
When does Proposition 19 help?Only when the home was the parent’s principal residence and becomes the heir’s principal residence within one year, with the exemption claimed in time. Renting it out generally fails that test.BOE LTA 2026/026 · Prop 19 Q&A ↗
What is the value cap?Factored base year value plus the adjusted allowance, $1,044,590 for transfers from 16 February 2025 to 15 February 2027. Value above the cap is added to the base.BOE LTA 2025/009 · $1,044,586 ↗
What if the heir later moves out?The exclusion is removed and the value enrolled is the date-of-transfer market value, factored forward, not the later market value.BOE LTA 2026/026 · Prop 19 Q&A ↗
How much does the homeowners’ exemption save?$7,000 off assessed value, about $80 to $90 a year at the Cupertino rate, and it must be claimed.Santa Clara County Assessor · homeowners' exemption ↗
When are the bills due?First instalment delinquent after 10 December, second after 10 April; a 10% penalty applies.Santa Clara County · FY2025-26 tax rate book ↗
02 / income tax

Income tax on rent and on sale

QuestionCurrent ruleSource
What is the basis of inherited property?Generally fair market value at the date of death, so a prompt sale produces little or no gain. A trust outside the estate can keep the old basis instead.IRS · inherited basis FAQ ↗ · IRS · Rev. Rul. 2023-2 ↗
Can the building be depreciated?Yes, over 27.5 years, on the building only; land is never depreciable. Gain on that depreciation is recaptured at up to 25% on sale.IRS · Publication 527 ↗
What is the home-sale exclusion?Up to $250,000 of gain, or $500,000 on a joint return, if the home was owned and used as a main home for 24 of the previous 60 months.IRS · Topic 701, sale of your home ↗
Are rental losses deductible?Generally passive; a $25,000 allowance phases out between $100,000 and $150,000 of modified adjusted gross income, and unused losses carry forward.IRS · Publication 527 ↗
Is there an extra investment tax?3.8% net investment income tax above $200,000 single or $250,000 joint, on the lesser of net investment income or the excess. Thresholds are not indexed.IRS · Topic 559, NIIT ↗
Does California tax capital gains differently?No; they are ordinary income, up to 13.3% at the top.CA State Controller · estate tax ↗
Is anything withheld when the house is sold?California withholds 3 1/3% of the sales price unless an exemption is certified before closing.California FTB · Form 593 ↗
Can the proceeds be rolled into another property?A like-kind exchange needs property held for business or investment, not a personal residence.IRS · Publication 551 ↗
