Property-tax rules

QuestionCurrent rule / limitationReference
What changes at transfer?Change in ownership can cause reassessment. The Proposition 13 factored base year value generally grows by the inflation factor, capped at 2%, absent reassessment or new construction; declining-market relief can apply.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
When can Proposition 19 help?Generally an eligible parent-child family-home transfer requires the transferor’s principal residence and the transferee to make it their principal residence within one year and continue occupancy. Special rules apply to other eligible transfers.California BOE · fact sheet ↗
How much value is protected?For 16 Feb 2025–15 Feb 2027 the additional allowance is approximately $1,044,590, rounded from the official amount. If fair market value exceeds factored base value plus the allowance, the excess is added to the factored value. The Board states the allowance is adjusted every other February 16; a county summary page describes a different cadence, so the Board’s Letters to Assessors govern. Later amounts are unknown.BOE · 2025/009 ↗ · Santa Clara County Assessor ↗
Does renting qualify?Renting the inherited home instead of making it the eligible transferee’s principal residence generally fails the family-home condition. Confirm facts with the assessor.California BOE · Prop 19 ↗

Estate, gift and income tax

ItemOfficial current informationCase-specific amountReference
Federal basic exclusion$15,000,000 for deaths in 2026; $15,480,000 in 2027. Prior taxable gifts and an available transferred spousal exclusion may change the available amount.— unverifiedIRS · 2027 adjustments ↗
Filing threshold versus taxable estateThe filing threshold generally tests the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts and certain exemption amounts. Deductions are relevant to taxable estate and tax, not a simple subtraction from the gross filing test.— unverifiedIRS · estate tax ↗
CaliforniaThe Controller says no California estate-tax return is required for deaths on or after 1 January 2005. Other taxes, and taxes of other jurisdictions if relevant, are separate.— unverifiedCA Controller · estate tax ↗
Income-tax basisInherited property generally receives date-of-death FMV basis, subject to exceptions and elections. One particular irrevocable grantor-trust fact pattern outside the estate did not receive an adjustment.— unverifiedIRS · Rev. Rul. 2023-2 ↗
Retirement accountsTaxable inherited IRA distributions may be income to the beneficiary; treatment and timing depend on account and beneficiary rules.— unverifiedIRS · retirement beneficiaries ↗
Federal home-sale exclusionUp to $250,000, or $500,000 for qualifying joint filers, subject to ownership, use and other conditions; it is not automatic.— unverifiedIRS · sale of home ↗
