Distribution of outcomes across the scenario bands (25 years)

The chart is the table above drawn as a distribution: for each path the bar spans the worst to the best of the four stress scenarios at the 25-year horizon, the diamond marks the baseline, and the ticks mark the crash and stagflation scenarios. A wide bar means the path is scenario-sensitive; a short bar means the scenario band changes little. These are illustrative model outputs on fictional example inputs — not forecasts, and no probability is attached to any scenario.

Reading the shape: the sell paths cluster at the high end with wide scenario spreads (they are fully exposed to the stock scenario), the live path is pulled down by the holding costs in every scenario, and the mixed paths sit between the two. The distribution is the honest form of the single-number comparison: there is no path whose worst case equals its best case. The stress shapes are stylised; see Black swans and the flagged notes.
