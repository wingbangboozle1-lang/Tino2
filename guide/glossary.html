property tax

Property-tax terms

TermMeaning, as the official source states itSource
Change in ownershipA transfer of a present interest substantially equal in value to a fee interest. The BOE page gives purchase or inheritance of a property as examples.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
Base year value (BYV)The market value established when a change in ownership or new construction occurs, unless an exclusion applies.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
Factored base year value (FBYV)The base year value adjusted each year by a California CPI-based inflation factor, not to exceed 2%.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
Assessed valueThe dollar value a County Assessor assigns to a property for property-tax purposes.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
Market valueThe price a property would bring to its owner if offered for sale on the open market.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
Proposition 8 decline-in-valueWhen market value falls below the FBYV on the January 1 lien date, the Assessor enrolls the lower market value. The assessed value can then rise by more than 2% a year, but never above the FBYV.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
Proposition 19Approved 3 Nov 2020. Limits which transfers of a family home keep the lower base year value. Current law requires the transferee to use the property as a principal residence, and it removes the exclusion for other real property. Effective 16 Feb 2021.California BOE · Prop 19 ↗
Homeowners’ exemptionAn exemption that reduces the assessed value subject to tax. The Proposition 19 table notes a filing deadline of one year after transfer.California BOE · Prop 19 ↗
inheritance

Inheritance and estate-tax terms

TermMeaning, as the official source states itSource
Fair market value at deathThe generally applicable basis of inherited property: its fair market value on the date of the decedent’s death.IRS · inherited basis ↗
Alternate valuation dateAn option, available only if an estate tax return is filed and the executor elects it, to use fair market value on a different date for basis.IRS · inherited basis ↗
Schedule A to Form 8971If the recipient receives this schedule, the recipient may be required to report a basis consistent with the estate-tax value.IRS · inherited basis ↗
Gross estateEverything the decedent owned or had certain interests in at death, valued at fair market value, including real estate and trusts.IRS · estate tax ↗
Filing thresholdThe amount above which an estate tax return is required, set by year of death. The IRS table lists $15,000,000 for 2026.IRS · estate tax ↗
Unified creditThe credit by which the computed estate tax is reduced.IRS · estate tax ↗
PortabilityFor a decedent survived by a spouse, an election on a timely filed return to pass unused exemption to the surviving spouse.IRS · estate tax ↗
Form 706The United States Estate (and Generation-Skipping Transfer) Tax Return.IRS · inherited basis ↗
sale and reporting

Home-sale and reporting terms

TermMeaning, as the official source states itSource
Home-sale exclusionUp to $250,000 of gain from the sale of a main home may be excluded, or up to $500,000 on a joint return, if the ownership and use tests are met.IRS · sale of home ↗
Ownership and use testsOwnership for at least 24 months, and use as a residence for at least 24 months, in the 5 years ending on the sale date.IRS · sale of home ↗
Installment saleA sale under a contract that provides for all or part of the price to be paid in a later year. The exclusion remains available.IRS · sale of home ↗
Form 1099-SThe informational return reporting real-estate proceeds. A sale reported on it must be reported on the return even if the gain is excludable.IRS · sale of home ↗
