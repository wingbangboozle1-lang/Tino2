Terms used in this guide

TermPlain meaningWhy it matters hereOfficial source
Basis (income-tax)The starting figure for gain or loss on a later sale. For inherited property the IRS FAQ gives the general rule as fair market value on the date of death, with an alternate-valuation exception.Decides the tax on any later sale of the inherited home. Assessed value is a different number.IRS · inherited basis ↗
Change in ownership (property tax)A transfer that can reset assessed value to current market value unless an exclusion applies.Determines whether the property-tax bill resets after the transfer.California BOE · Prop 19 ↗
Change-in-ownership statementThe signed form filed with the county recorder or assessor for a transfer of real property. For a transfer by reason of death through a trust, it is filed within 150 days after the date of death.Sets a hard filing date that the trustee or transferee must meet.CA Revenue & Taxation Code §480 ↗
Date of deathThe date the owner died.Starts the deadlines on the Timeline page.IRS · estate tax ↗
Dollar-cost averagingInvesting equal amounts at regular intervals regardless of market ups and downs; the investor buys more shares when prices are low and fewer when they are high.The phased path in the entry-risk test is a form of dollar-cost averaging.SEC Investor.gov · dollar-cost averaging ↗
Family-home transfer (Proposition 19)A transfer of a principal residence from parent to child, where the child makes it a principal residence. The BOE page sets a value limit built from the factored base-year value plus an allowance. The allowance is adjusted over time; the current amount is not stated here (see Flagged for Review).The main route to limited reassessment for an inherited home. The guide does not conclude that it applies.California BOE · Prop 19 ↗
Federal filing thresholdThe gross-estate amount above which a federal estate tax return is required. The IRS page lists $15,000,000 for a 2026 death.Determines whether a federal return is needed at all.IRS · estate tax ↗ · IRS · Instructions for Form 706 ↗
Form 706The federal estate and generation-skipping transfer tax return. The executor uses it to figure the estate tax.The return the CPA and attorney must decide whether to file.IRS · About Form 706 ↗
Form 1041The income-tax return for a decedent’s estate or trust. It reports income, deductions, gains and distributions to beneficiaries.Governs trust and estate income once the property is held outside the decedent.IRS · About Form 1041 ↗
Form 4768The application for an automatic 6-month extension of time to file Form 706.Used to request the automatic 6-month extension of time to file. It is the form the IRS names for that request.IRS · Instructions for Form 706 ↗
Peak-to-trough declineThe fall from a market’s highest level to its lowest level before the next recovery. Example: the 1929 decline the Federal Reserve reports as 89 percent.The measure used to build the 1929 and 2008 crash shapes.Federal Reserve History · Stock Market Crash of 1929 ↗
Probate Code §16061.7 noticeA trustee notice to beneficiaries and heirs, due not later than 60 days following the triggering event, subject to the section’s exceptions.Sets the notice clock that runs in parallel with the estate and tax filings.California Probate Code §16061.7 ↗
ReassessmentSetting a new taxable value for property. The BOE guidance states that a family home that stops being the owner’s principal residence receives a new taxable value.The step that can move the property-tax line sharply after the transfer.California BOE · Prop 19 ↗
Residential rental propertyIRS Publication 527 is titled Residential Rental Property; the guide does not restate its rules.Relevant to Property B, which the planning premise treats as rented.IRS · residential rental property ↗
Treasury par yield (Constant Maturity)The yield the U.S. Treasury publishes for a given maturity, derived from the daily par yield curve. Used here for the 3-month and 2-year rates.The observed parking yields on 13 November 2026 come from this series.U.S. Treasury · daily par yield curve, November 2026 ↗

Each term links to an official page. Definitions are plain-language summaries of the page named in the source column, not legal advice. Reinvestment risk is used on the Black swans page as a label; no official definition has been captured yet, so it is not defined here. See Flagged for Review.
