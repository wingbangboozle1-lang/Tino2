Live in it, rent it, or sell it.

A three-way comparison with year-by-year figures, break-even rates and a grid showing where the ranking changes.

Anonymous by designOfficial sources linkedProjections are estimates
These are planning estimates, not a recommendation for a specific household. The two property values are owner estimates rounded to $1,000. Tax rates are official. Every operating cost is a stated assumption listed on the method page. Change any of them below and the tables recompute in your browser.
01 / property tax at transfer

03 / break-even

Break-even home-price growth

The rate at which holding A overtakes selling it, with every other assumption unchanged. The recorded 1975-2025 nominal growth for Santa Clara County was 7.01% a year and the real rate was 2.98%. The central projection uses 3.6%, inside the CBO national path.
