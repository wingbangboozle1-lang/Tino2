The hold strategies include a hypothetical sale at the endpoint and assumed gain tax. Sale proceeds earn a constant after-tax return proxy; there is no additional portfolio liquidation tax. Retained-property cash flows are accumulated using the same proxy. Sell’s first-year figure is a portfolio-value change proxy, not spendable distributions. Living costs are treated as funded externally and compounded. No estate-tax allocation or value of housing services is included. These omissions can change the ordering.
