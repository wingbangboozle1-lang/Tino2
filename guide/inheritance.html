Fifteen steps from trust to decision

#WhenStepWhy it mattersDeadlineSource
1BeforeLocate the trust, deed and property scheduleThe signed trust, any amendments, the deed and the schedule of assets establish who holds title and who may sell. A trust does not by itself prove a basis adjustment.Months to years aheadIRS · Rev. Rul. 2023-2 ↗
2BeforeConfirm the trust typeA revocable trust that is included in the estate generally resets basis. Property in an irrevocable trust outside the estate may keep the old basis, which removes the main reason selling beats holding.Before relying on any projectionIRS · Rev. Rul. 2023-2 ↗

4At deathObtain the date-of-death valuationA qualified appraisal at the date of death sets both the income-tax basis and the reassessed value.ImmediatelyIRS · inherited basis FAQ ↗

6Within 150 daysFile the change in ownership statementThe trustee or transferee files with the county; a penalty applies for failing to respond to a written request.150 daysCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §480 ↗
7Within 1 yearClaim the homeowners’ exemptionRequired for the Proposition 19 exclusion to be timely, and worth about $90 a year on its own.1 yearBOE LTA 2026/026 · Prop 19 Q&A ↗
8Within 3 yearsFile the exclusion claimForm BOE-19-P must be filed within three years or before a transfer to a third party. Late claims get prospective relief only.3 yearsSanta Clara County Assessor · parent-child transfer ↗
9Within 9 monthsDecide on Form 706The federal return is due nine months after death, extendable six. Filing is also how portability is elected, so it can matter even when no tax is due.9 monthsIRS · estate tax FAQ ↗
10Before sellingGet the title and lien pictureLiens, reverse mortgages, co-beneficiaries and equalisation duties can each block or reshape a sale.Before listingCalifornia Probate Code §16061.7 ↗
11Before sellingModel the sale costsCommission, escrow, title and transfer costs are modelled at 6%; they come off the top before any comparison to holding.Before listingIRS · Topic 701, sale of your home ↗
12At saleCheck the withholding ruleCalifornia withholds 3 1/3% of the price unless an exemption is certified before closing, which affects cash available at settlement.At closingCalifornia FTB · Form 593 ↗
13If rentingSet up the rental properlyLand versus building allocation, depreciation schedule, local rent rules and insurance form all have to be in place before the first tenant.Before the first leaseIRS · Publication 527 ↗
14If rentingVerify the insurance formA landlord policy differs from a homeowners policy, and both generally exclude earth movement.Before the first leaseCA Dept of Insurance · earthquake cover ↗
15Every yearRecheck the law and the numbersThe Proposition 19 allowance steps every second February, and the federal exclusion and rent rules change.AnnuallyBOE LTA 2025/009 · $1,044,586 ↗
