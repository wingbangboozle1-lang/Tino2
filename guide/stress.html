Black-swan economic conditions.

The real 1976-2025 record, five named shocks and 2,000 resampled thirty-year histories, because growth and inflation are never consistent.

Anonymous by designOfficial sources linkedProjections are estimates
Growth is never a straight line. A single 3.6% growth assumption over 50 years is a convenience, not a forecast. This page replaces it with the actual joint record of California inflation and county house prices from 1976 to 2025, replayed two ways.

The county index fell 25.4% between 2006 and 2011 and went 9 years without a new high. Over the full 1975-2025 period it rose 7.0% a year in nominal terms and 3.0% in real terms. The model uses 3.6% nominal, which sits above the real median and below the nominal median.

03 / resampled history

2,000 resampled histories

The record contains only one 1975-2025 window, which is too few to judge risk. Instead the 30-year joint history of annual inflation and house-price change is resampled in five-year blocks, 2,000 times, and each draw is run through the same model. Outcomes are deflated to constant purchasing power.
