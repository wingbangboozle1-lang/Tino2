Same-endpoint outcomes

The starting 2045 property value, rent, costs, sale fee and tax rates come from the default fictional example, not the comparison page’s current control selections. After 2045, rent grows 2.5% and vacancy is 5% except during listed shocks. Water and insurance follow each path’s inflation, plus the listed premium shocks; maintenance follows modeled home value. B is treated the same across choices and is not included. No estate-tax payment or financing is modeled.

Nominal amounts are rounded to $10. Real figures are divided by cumulative inflation and expressed in 2045 dollars. Living results omit the value of housing services; negative reserves assume externally funded costs.

Audit every year

Expand a path to inspect all 50 annual rates and outputs. The model caps annual factor growth at 2% and uses the lesser of market value and factored base as a simplified decline-in-value proxy; actual lien dates, assessment appeals, new construction and supplemental bills are not modeled. The disaster repair is charged to both property-holding strategies. Each portfolio rate is a hypothetical net proxy with no separate liquidation tax. Income tax on positive rental cash is a flat scenario; depreciation and recapture are omitted.
