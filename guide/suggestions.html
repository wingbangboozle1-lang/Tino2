S28MediumRun the private model on ranges rather than pointsReplace each single assumption with the low and high of the band the Real conditions page now publishes for it, and report both outcomes wherever a single number appears today.A point estimate invites false precision about an event years away; a band states the uncertainty honestly.Congressional Budget Office ↗— record decision and date offline
S29MediumMove the privacy check to the door, not only to CIRun scripts/privacy-gate.py in a pre-commit or pre-receive hook so an identifier or an un-rounded amount never reaches a pushed branch.CI catches the problem after the push, which is when a search index can already see it.GitHub · sensitive data removal ↗— record decision and date offline
S30HighBucket the per-parcel assessment history, do not only round it$10 rounding does not defeat an exact-string search when a figure already ends in a zero. S31HighWrite the parking leg into the policy as a ladder, not a rateAdopt the form the Black swans page now tests: proceeds held in a 3-month/2-year Treasury ladder for 24 to 36 months and phased in over 12, with the note leg sized only for money that will not be needed before month 24.Bills alone reinvest the whole pot at whatever the crash did to short rates; the tested table shows the gap on the same wait.TreasuryDirect · Treasury bills ↗— record decision and date offline
S32MediumReplace the assumed 2008 regain month with an official seriesThe entry-risk test carries 66 months as a labelled assumption because no official page re-opened in this review states when the S&P 500 regained its October 2007 level. Find a primary series, record it in the figure ledger and rebuild.One row of the Black swans page rests on an assumption where the 1929 row rests on the record; the difference should not be invisible.Federal Reserve History · The Great Recession ↗— record decision and date offline
S33HighWrite the parking yield as a range in the investing policy, not as today’s rateThe recorded test shows a flat current-rate assumption flatters a 24-month park by about $17,610 per $100,000 at the 2008 entry, because bill yields fell from 1.40% to 0.15% during that parking window. State the policy as a yield band with a reinvestment date, and re-check the published yields at each roll.A policy written against a spot rate overstates what the parked money will earn exactly when a crash makes parking look attractive.FRED · 3-month Treasury bill (DGS3MO) ↗— record decision and date offline
S34MediumChoose the parking maturity once, in writing, and stop re-deciding it monthlyOn the record, holding parked proceeds at the 2-year note yield rather than rolling 3-month bills changed the ten-year result by at most about $11,620 per $100,000 and by roughly $2,700 at the median, while the entry date moved the result by tens of thousands. Pick one ladder in the policy, note the trade-off, and do not treat it as a market call.It removes an argument that does not matter from a decision that does, and it keeps the plan from drifting into a rate forecast.TreasuryDirect · Treasury notes ↗— record decision and date offline
S35MediumAdd the entry-year test to the annual re-runThe recommendation-level question is not only what the plan earns but when the money goes in. Each year, re-run the Black swans entry test on the current data extract and record whether the conclusion moved, alongside the property and tax inputs.Entry timing is the part of the sell path a household controls, and its evidence base changes as history accumulates.FRED · 2-year Treasury note (DGS2) ↗— record decision and date offline
S36LowDecide in writing whether a loss at the worst point is acceptableThe recorded test prices waiting at a median of about −$42,860 per $100,000 over ten years in exchange for a shallower drawdown that only pays off in the minority of entries. Agree the trade-off before the proceeds arrive, so the choice is not made under the influence of whichever month the money lands in.It is the criterion the phasing decision actually turns on, and it cannot be read off a projection.SEC · diversification ↗— record decision and date offline
