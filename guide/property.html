Regulator survey

Insurance research — regulator survey, not a quote

The California Department of Insurance publishes a premium-comparison survey (effective 1 May 2026; read 12 Nov 2026) and states that it is not a quote. The profiles are homeowners policies with one million dollars of dwelling cover; the median is about 2.69 per $1,000 of cover for the Cupertino survey location and about 3.35 per $1,000 for the San Francisco–Sunset survey location, so a larger rebuild limit scales roughly with cover. The regulator cannot issue a policy. Property B is a three-unit building: the homeowners survey is the wrong product for it and its row is a flagged proxy.

Property A · tax rate area and the published rate

The brief asks for the property’s tax rate area and the rate published for it. The county’s own glossary supplies the public part of the answer; the parcel-specific part is printed on each secured tax bill and stays a private-record question (discovery register D08), because publishing the parcel’s rate area together with its financial history would narrow the anonymity the rounding rule protects.

Reading the parcel’s TRA number and its exact ad valorem rate off the county bill replaces the implied rates in both models; that is suggestion N07 and the flagged item about tax-rate-area rates. The Assessor’s glossary is the official source for the definitions; the county’s rate compilation for each fiscal year is where every TRA’s exact rate is published, and it is read with the bill.

Proposition H parcel tax — official schedule for B

San Francisco voters approved Proposition H on 3 November 2026. SFMTA publishes the FY 2027–28 annual amounts by parcel type and taxable building area, bills the first tax on fiscal year 2027–28 bills mailed in autumn 2027, and indexes the amounts with inflation from FY 2028–29. The measure runs from 1 July 2027 to 30 June 2042. The rows below are the published amounts; they are official rates, not estimates.

Why B’s amount is blank: the tax depends on the parcel type the Assessor-Recorder uses and on taxable building area. Neither can be read from a public page, so no figure is computed here. Exemptions and reductions exist for qualifying senior owners, SRO parcels and parcels on which no property tax is levied; the exemption application deadline is 1 June for the coming fiscal year. The measure is not included in the synthetic operating table on the comparison page, so that table understates B’s costs from FY 2027–28 onward.
