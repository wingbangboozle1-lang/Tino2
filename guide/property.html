Proposition H parcel tax — official schedule for B

San Francisco voters approved Proposition H on 3 November 2026. SFMTA publishes the FY 2027–28 annual amounts by parcel type and taxable building area, bills the first tax on fiscal year 2027–28 bills mailed in autumn 2027, and indexes the amounts with inflation from FY 2028–29. The measure runs from 1 July 2027 to 30 June 2042. The rows below are the published amounts; they are official rates, not estimates.

Why B’s amount is blank: the tax depends on the parcel type the Assessor-Recorder uses and on taxable building area. Neither can be read from a public page, so no figure is computed here. Exemptions and reductions exist for qualifying senior owners, SRO parcels and parcels on which no property tax is levied; the exemption application deadline is 1 June for the coming fiscal year. The measure is not included in the synthetic operating table on the comparison page, so that table understates B’s costs from FY 2027–28 onward.
