Two properties, two sets of facts.

What is known, what is estimated, what is projected, and what still has to be checked against a real bill.

Anonymous by designOfficial sources linkedProjections are estimates
Two different things are being mixed here, on purpose but never blended. Property A is a future inheritance. Property B is already owned. B does not belong in the decedent’s estate unless a specific fact creates an interest, and B’s rent continues whatever happens to A. The tables below keep them apart.

Cupertino bill does not match the published rateThe FY2025-26 components sum to 1.1597% of assessed value, which is about $6,260 on a $540,000 assessment.
