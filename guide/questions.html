Questions, answers and discovery

PriorityQuestionVerified answerHow to discover privatelyDecision impactOfficial framework
1Who owns A, and does the signed trust actually hold title?— unverifiedRead deed, trust schedule and amendments with the trustee.Changes authority to transfer or sell.California RTC §480 ↗
1What type of trust, what powers and which beneficiaries?— unverifiedObtain signed trust and amendments privately; ask counsel about distribution powers.Affects allocation, timing, tax and contest risk.California Probate Code §16061.7 ↗
1Does A qualify for an income-tax basis adjustment?— unverifiedAsk a tax lawyer to assess title, estate inclusion and trust facts.An unadjusted basis can change the sale analysis materially.IRS · Rev. Rul. 2023-2 ↗
1Was A the transferor’s principal residence and can an eligible recipient occupy it on time?— unverifiedDocument residence and relationship; ask the county assessor about special circumstances.Determines eligibility for family-home reassessment relief.California BOE · fact sheet ↗
1What is the complete gross estate, prior taxable gifts, debts and available spousal exclusion?— unverifiedPrepare a confidential estate inventory with advisers; include all relevant interests.Determines filing and estate tax; no actual headroom can be calculated yet.IRS · estate tax ↗
1What is the property’s date-of-death fair market value?— unverifiedObtain a qualified appraisal at the relevant date; retain private documentation.Sets the usual basis and affects reassessment and sale proceeds.IRS · inherited basis ↗
1Are there co-beneficiaries, equalization duties, debts or liens?— unverifiedReview trust, loans, liens and distribution terms privately.Could constrain the ability or timing to keep or sell.California Probate Code §16061.7 ↗
1What are the actual assessed values and parcel-specific tax rates?— unverifiedRead county bill and tax-rate-area details for each property.A simple percentage misses direct charges and reassessment timing.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
1What are the dwelling replacement costs and actual insurance quotes?— unverifiedGet owner-occupied and rental/owner-occupied-multiunit quotes as applicable.Premium, deductible, exclusions and vacancy terms alter cash flow and risk.CA Department of Insurance ↗
1How much are lawful market rent, vacancy and management costs for A?— unverifiedGet independent current comparables and professional management estimates.Rental economics and legal limitations can change the ranking.HUD · small-area rent data ↗
1What are the current leases and rent-control status for B?— unverifiedReview leases, building history and Rent Board rules privately.Income and lawful increases cannot be inferred from a benchmark.SF Rent Board · current rates ↗
2Who pays utilities and what are actual bills?— unverifiedReview a year of bills, meter counts, provider and lease terms.Water, sewer and electricity are not fixed by the ZIP code.City of Cupertino · water ↗
2What solar tariff and interconnection date apply?— unverifiedReview interconnection agreement and annual true-up privately.Future export credits and fixed charges may differ.PG&E · solar billing ↗
2What repairs and capital improvements are likely?— unverifiedObtain inspections, multi-year invoices and contractor estimates.Maintenance, capital basis and one-off risks affect both costs and tax.IRS · residential rental property ↗
2What is land versus building value if A is rented?— unverifiedUse a defensible appraisal and tax preparer’s depreciation schedule.Depreciation and later recapture can materially change the comparison.IRS · residential rental property ↗
2What are the income, brackets and potential passive-loss limits?— unverifiedPrepare confidential taxpayer-specific scenarios with a CPA.Flat scenario rates are not the actual combined tax.IRS · residential rental property ↗
2What is the type and beneficiary treatment of inherited cash or retirement accounts?— unverifiedInventory account types and designations privately with an adviser.IRA distributions and cash or securities have different tax treatment.IRS · retirement beneficiaries ↗
