A live, rent or sell decision cannot reasonably be made today, because every Priority 1 item is still open and each one is a fact that only the household’s own records and advisers can supply. What is settled is the rule set, which is what makes a private comparison possible at all. Close the Priority 1 items first — the suggestions register lists the route for each one.

Questions, answers and discovery

1Who owns A, and does the signed trust actually hold title?Rule known: where an interest transfers by reason of death, including through a trust, the trustee or transferee files the change-in-ownership statement with the county recorder or assessor within 150 days of death; where the property is probated, the statement is filed with the inventory and appraisal. That fixes who must file, not who holds title.— unverifiedRead deed, trust schedule and amendments with the trustee.Changes authority to transfer or sell.California RTC §480 ↗
1What type of trust, what powers and which beneficiaries?Rule known: a trustee must serve written notification on the beneficiaries and heirs of a deceased settlor within 60 days of specified events, including a revocable trust becoming irrevocable because of a settlor’s death. The trust instrument, not the statute, sets the powers and the distribution timetable.— unverifiedObtain signed trust and amendments privately; ask counsel about distribution powers.Affects allocation, timing, tax and contest risk.California Probate Code §16061.7 ↗
1Does A qualify for an income-tax basis adjustment?Rule known: inherited property generally takes its basis in the fair market value at the decedent’s death. Where property is not includible in the decedent’s gross estate, no basis adjustment applies — the IRS so held for the irrevocable-trust fact pattern in Rev. Rul. 2023-2.— unverifiedAsk a tax lawyer to assess title, estate inclusion and trust facts.An unadjusted basis can change the sale analysis materially.IRS · Rev. Rul. 2023-2 ↗
1Was A the transferor’s principal residence and can an eligible recipient occupy it on time?Rule known: for a parent–child family-home transfer the transferee must make the home their principal residence within one year and claim the homeowners’ or disabled veterans’ exemption within that year, or the exclusion starts in the year the claim is filed. Renting the home out does not satisfy the family-home condition.— unverifiedDocument residence and relationship; ask the county assessor about special circumstances.Determines eligibility for family-home reassessment relief.California BOE · fact sheet ↗
1What is the complete gross estate, prior taxable gifts, debts and available spousal exclusion?Rule known: a return is required only if the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts and specific gift tax exemption exceeds the filing threshold, which is $15,000,000 for a 2026 death. Deductions bear on the taxable estate, not on that filing test. California requires no estate-tax return for deaths on or after 1 January 2005.— unverifiedPrepare a confidential estate inventory with advisers; include all relevant interests.Determines filing and estate tax; no actual headroom can be calculated yet.IRS · estate tax ↗
1What is the property’s date-of-death fair market value?Rule known: the basis is generally the fair market value at the date of death, or at the alternate valuation date if the executor elects it on Form 706. A qualified appraisal is how that value is established and documented.— unverifiedObtain a qualified appraisal at the relevant date; retain private documentation.Sets the usual basis and affects reassessment and sale proceeds.IRS · inherited basis ↗
1Are there co-beneficiaries, equalization duties, debts or liens?Rule known: the trust instrument, the lien records and the probate rules decide who may take property free of claims and when. No statute published on this site answers that for a particular trust.— unverifiedReview trust, loans, liens and distribution terms privately.Could constrain the ability or timing to keep or sell.California Probate Code §16061.7 ↗
1What are the actual assessed values and parcel-specific tax rates?Rule known: Proposition 13 caps annual assessed-value growth at 2% and limits the property tax rate to 1% plus voter-approved debt; a change in ownership generally sets base year value to market value, subject to exclusions. Where market value falls below the factored base year value, decline-in-value relief applies.— unverifiedRead county bill and tax-rate-area details for each property.A simple percentage misses direct charges and reassessment timing.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
1What are the dwelling replacement costs and actual insurance quotes?Rule known: the Department of Insurance publishes premium-comparison surveys and states on the page that they are not quotes; it is a regulator and cannot issue one. Standard homeowners policies exclude earthquake damage, with a separate California Earthquake Authority policy available.— unverifiedGet owner-occupied and rental/owner-occupied-multiunit quotes as applicable.Premium, deductible, exclusions and vacancy terms alter cash flow and risk.CA Department of Insurance ↗
1How much are lawful market rent, vacancy and management costs for A?Rule known: HUD small-area fair market rents are ZIP-code benchmarks calculated for Housing Choice Voucher payment standards. They are not a rent quote and do not determine a lawful rent.— unverifiedGet independent current comparables and professional management estimates.Rental economics and legal limitations can change the ranking.HUD · small-area rent data ↗
1What are the current leases and rent-control status for B?Rule known: for covered San Francisco units the general annual allowable increase is 1.6% from 1 March 2026 through 28 February 2027. Board of Supervisors File 261020 would limit combined banked and capital-improvement increases, but the Rent Board’s 9 November 2026 notice states it has not been enacted.— unverifiedReview leases, building history and Rent Board rules privately.Income and lawful increases cannot be inferred from a benchmark.SF Rent Board · current rates ↗
2Who pays utilities and what are actual bills?Rule known: Cupertino supplies water through three separate systems — California Water Services, San Jose Water Company and the city-operated system — so a ZIP code does not identify the provider or the rate schedule.— unverifiedReview a year of bills, meter counts, provider and lease terms.Water, sewer and electricity are not fixed by the ZIP code.City of Cupertino · water ↗
2What solar tariff and interconnection date apply?Rule known: PG&E’s Solar Billing Plan produces a monthly statement and an annual True-Up statement. The tariff a system keeps and the value of any export credit depend on when the system was authorised to operate, so a monthly credit is not assured.— unverifiedReview interconnection agreement and annual true-up privately.Future export credits and fixed charges may differ.PG&E · solar billing ↗
2What repairs and capital improvements are likely?Rule known: rental expenses are deductible only if the property is rented or held for production of income; capital work is generally depreciated rather than deducted, and whether an item is a repair or an improvement is a question of fact that the records decide.— unverifiedObtain inspections, multi-year invoices and contractor estimates.Maintenance, capital basis and one-off risks affect both costs and tax.IRS · residential rental property ↗
2What is land versus building value if A is rented?Rule known: land is not depreciable; the building portion is. The allocation between them is an appraisal and tax-preparer question, not a published figure.— unverifiedUse a defensible appraisal and tax preparer’s depreciation schedule.Depreciation and later recapture can materially change the comparison.IRS · residential rental property ↗
2What are the income, brackets and potential passive-loss limits?Rule known: the comparison page uses a flat scenario rate. The actual tax follows the taxpayer’s own brackets, other income and the passive-loss rules in Publication 527.— unverifiedPrepare confidential taxpayer-specific scenarios with a CPA.Flat scenario rates are not the actual combined tax.IRS · residential rental property ↗
2What is the type and beneficiary treatment of inherited cash or retirement accounts?Rule known: beneficiaries of an IRA and most plans may take a lump-sum distribution at any time and must include taxable distributions in gross income; a designated beneficiary who is not an eligible designated beneficiary follows the 10-year rule. Cash and securities carry no equivalent beneficiary rule.— unverifiedInventory account types and designations privately with an adviser.IRA distributions and cash or securities have different tax treatment.IRS · retirement beneficiaries ↗
2What is the desired living arrangement and the value of housing services?Rule known: none — no official source governs this question. It is a household preference, and the financial ranking on the comparison page deliberately omits the value of housing services.— preference not establishedCompare nonfinancial needs, work, accessibility and alternate housing privately.A financial ranking omits the benefit of living in a home.SEC · diversification ↗
2How would sale proceeds be invested, and how much liquidity is needed?Rule known: FDIC insurance covers depositor accounts dollar-for-dollar up to the limit; for a trust owner with five or more beneficiaries that limit is $1,250,000 per owner for all trust accounts held at the same bank. Diversification and asset allocation can reduce risk but do not guarantee returns.— preference not establishedSet an investment policy, risk tolerance and deposit-insurance plan.Returns, tax drag and sequence risk are assumptions, not guarantees.FDIC · insured deposits ↗
3What laws, valuation and insurance conditions will apply at the event?Rule known: the official baseline is CBO’s February 2026 outlook for 2026–2036, in which PCE inflation returns to about 2% by 2030. It is a ten-year budget outlook, not a 50-year housing or portfolio forecast, and future law is unknown.— not knowable yetRecheck official sources regularly and immediately before a decision.Future law and prices can make today’s model obsolete.Congressional Budget Office ↗
