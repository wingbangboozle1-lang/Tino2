Before a transferLocate the signed trust, amendments, deed, property schedule, beneficiary designations and trustee powers. Privately confirm ownership and estate inclusion.A trust does not by itself prove a particular basis adjustment or authority to sell.IRS · Rev. Rul. 2023-2 ↗
At the triggering eventSuccessor trustee accepts the role, safeguards the home, arranges insurance and retains certified records through private channels.Review occupancy, coverage, liens, beneficiaries and required notices with counsel.California Probate Code §16061.7 ↗
Generally within 60 days when requiredProvide statutory trustee notification to required recipients after an applicable triggering event; statutory timing and exceptions depend on the facts.Do not assume every trust triggers the same notice or deadline.California Probate Code §16061.7 ↗
Within 150 days for qualifying nonprobate death transfersTrustee or transferee files the change-in-ownership statement with the county where required. For probate, the statute has a different inventory-and-appraisal deadline.Check the precise form, dates and county instructions.California RTC §480 ↗
Within one year, if seeking family-home reliefAn eligible transferee must move into the home within one year; timely file the homeowners’ or disabled veterans’ exemption to obtain exclusion from the transfer date.Delayed exemption claims can have prospective effect; confirm current rules and special cases.California BOE · fact sheet ↗
Claim the exclusionFile BOE-19-P or applicable claim generally within three years and before transfer to a third party. A claim can also be timely within six months of the applicable supplemental or escape assessment notice.A missed or late filing can affect when relief begins.California BOE · fact sheet ↗
Value and accountArrange qualified date-of-death valuations; list includible assets, debts, prior gifts, distributions and liquidity.Basis and estate filing tests differ; already-owned heir assets are not automatically the decedent’s.IRS · estate tax ↗
By applicable federal deadlineDetermine if Form 706 is required or desirable for portability; check Form 1041 and final income returns.An extension to file is not automatically an extension to pay; get advice.IRS · estate tax ↗
Only after the reviewCompare sale, rental and occupancy on equal time horizons and after documented costs and taxes; follow trust authority and local rental rules.Avoid irreversible action solely on illustrative projections.IRS · residential rental property ↗
