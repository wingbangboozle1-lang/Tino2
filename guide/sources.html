How each official figure was checked

CheckWhat it doesWhat it does not doOfficial context
Machine-readable ledgerEvery official rule figure the guide publishes is recorded in a public ledger with its source URL, the exact published value, the form shown on the site, and the date it was re-opened.It does not prove the value is still current on the day you read it, and it does not verify any household figure.IRS · 2027 adjustments ↗
Build gateThe build fails if a seven-figure amount appears on a published page without a ledger record, or if a ledger record is marked verified without an official https source.It cannot detect a value that was verified once and has since changed at the agency.California BOE · fact sheet ↗
Rounding disclosureWhere a published amount is rounded to the nearest $10, the page says so and the ledger keeps the exact official value so a reader can match it.Rounding does not anonymize: a multi-year series can be matched even after rounding.BOE · Prop 13 explanation ↗
Deliberately unverifiedAn item that could not be confirmed at an official source is left blank on the page and recorded as unverified in the ledger.It stays open until someone opens the official document and records it.GitHub · sensitive data removal ↗
