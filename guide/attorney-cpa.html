Attorney & CPA briefing sheet · Inheritance decision guide · reviewed 14 Nov 2026

For [ATTORNEY] and [CPA]. A structure built on invented figures. The household’s own facts go to counsel privately under placeholders such as [DECEDENT], [TRUSTEE], [PROPERTY A] and [PROPERTY B]. Educational, not legal or tax advice.

Facts to confirm privately

  • Date of death, and the date-of-death value of [PROPERTY A] from a qualified appraiser (see IRS · inherited basis ↗).
  • Whether [PROPERTY A] is titled in [TRUSTEE]’s trust, and what the trust allows for a sale of the residence (see California Probate Code §16061.7 ↗).
  • Title, leases and unit status of [PROPERTY B], which the planning premise treats as two rented units and one available to the owner (see Estate-rule context ↗).
  • Gross estate, adjusted taxable gifts and any elections, compared with the federal filing threshold (see IRS · Instructions for Form 706 ↗).

Questions for the attorney

  • Does the trust allow sale of [PROPERTY A], or does it require another distribution? Which trust clauses control?
  • Who files the change-in-ownership statement for [PROPERTY A], and by when? The statutory window for a transfer through a trust is 150 days from death (CA Revenue &amp; Taxation Code §480 ↗).
  • Does a trustee notification apply, and what is the 60-day service date? (California Probate Code §16061.7 ↗)
  • Does any interest in [PROPERTY B] fall within the decedent’s estate, given how it is titled? (Estate-rule context ↗)

Questions for the CPA

  • Confirm the income-tax basis of [PROPERTY A]: the IRS FAQ gives the general rule (fair market value on the date of death) and the alternate-valuation exception. (IRS · inherited basis ↗)
  • Should a Form 706 be filed? If the return is not ready, the Form 4768 extension is 6 months from the 9-month due date (IRS · Instructions for Form 706 ↗).
  • Which returns does the trust or estate file, and on what dates? The Form 1041 due date is still to be confirmed from IRS text (IRS · About Form 1041 ↗).
  • What rental income and depreciation rules apply to [PROPERTY B]? (IRS · residential rental property ↗)
  • Which Proposition 19 claims apply to the transfer, and their filing periods? The periods are not yet confirmed from BOE text. (California BOE · Prop 19 ↗)

What would change this advice

  • A different trust clause, title, or basis answer from counsel or the CPA (see the questions above).
  • A different filing position for the estate, or a federal threshold change after the date of death.
  • A different parking yield, or a different post-trough growth view; the test shows the sensitivity, not a forecast.
