## PG&E electricity — solar / NEM (pge.com) — official
- Solar customers receive a monthly NEM Electric Statement and an annual True-Up statement (12-month cycle from interconnection date).
- Monthly bill includes a Base Services Charge (grid connection, billing, programs) plus any gas/non-energy charges; solar generation earns credits that offset grid energy charges month-to-month.
- Annual True-Up reconciles all charges and credits; any remaining surplus may receive Net Surplus Compensation; residual credits typically reset to zero.
- New residential solar customers are on the Solar Billing Plan (NEM 3.0 successor structure) with monthly billing and annual credit settlement.
- Owner's planning figure: usually no electricity cost; solar generates ≈ $10–$20/month credit — structurally consistent with PG&E's NEM description, but the specific dollar amount is owner-provided and unverified.
- Sources: https://www.pge.com/en/account/billing-and-assistance/understand-your-bill/solar-bill.html ; https://www.pge.com/en/account/billing-and-assistance/understand-your-bill/net-energy-metering-bill.html ; https://www.pge.com/assets/pge/localized/en/docs/clean-energy/solar/pge-solar-billing-plan-guide.pdf
