# California Estate / Inheritance Tax — Research Record

## Summary (verified against CA sources)
- California has NO state estate tax and NO state inheritance tax. (California's old "pick-up" estate tax was tied to a federal state-death-tax credit that Congress repealed effective 2005; it collects nothing today. Confirmed by California Franchise Tax Board analysis.)
- Inherited cash and property are not taxable income to the recipient under California law (FTB).
- When inherited property is later SOLD, California taxes any gain above the stepped-up basis as ordinary income (California has no separate capital gains rate; top marginal rate up to 13.3%).
- California Form 593: escrow withholding of 3.33% of gross sale price on California real estate sales; heirs may file Form 593 to claim an exemption when there is no gain (e.g., sale at stepped-up basis).

## Source links (official)
1. California Franchise Tax Board — "Gifts and Inheritance": California has no inheritance tax; inherited assets are not taxable income to the recipient. https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/income-types/gifts-and-inheritance.html
2. California Franchise Tax Board / SB 378 analysis (via state tax summary): California's estate tax statute remains on the book but is keyed to the repealed federal credit and collects zero. https://www.ftb.ca.gov (general FTB site)
3. California Department of Tax and Fee Administration / FTB — Form 593 (Real Estate Withholding): 3.33% withholding on sale of California real property; exemption certificates available. https://www.ftb.ca.gov (search Form 593)
