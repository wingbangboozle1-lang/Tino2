# California Proposition 13 & Proposition 19 — Research Record

## Proposition 13 (verified, California Constitution Article XIII A; CA State Board of Equalization)
- Base property tax rate limited to 1% of assessed value, plus the rate necessary to fund local voter-approved bonded indebtedness.
- Assessed value = base year value (set at purchase/change in ownership or new construction) adjusted annually by inflation factor, capped at 2% per year ("factored base year value").
- Reassessment to market value occurs on change of ownership or completion of new construction.
- Sources:
  - BOE, "How Property Is Assessed" (TRA Information Sheet): https://boe.ca.gov/pdf/pub800-10.pdf
  - BOE, Letter to Assessors (Prop 13 overview): https://boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta25023.pdf
  - Santa Clara County Assessor — Understanding Proposition 13: https://asr.santaclaracounty.gov/faq/understanding-proposition-13

## Proposition 19 (verified, California State Board of Equalization — boe.ca.gov/prop19/)
- Effective for transfers on or after February 16, 2021.
- Parent-child (and grandparent-grandchild) reassessment exclusion now limited to the family home (primary residence of BOTH transferor and transferee).
- The child must use the home as their primary residence within ONE YEAR of transfer and claim the homeowner's exemption.
- File Claim for Reassessment Exclusion for Transfer Between Parent and Child (Form BOE-19-P) with the county assessor within 3 years of date of death/transfer, or before transfer to a third party, whichever is earlier.
- Value cap: the exclusion covers the parent's factored base year value PLUS an inflation-adjusted amount. For transfers February 16, 2025 – February 15, 2027: $1,044,586 (BOE-adjusted). Prior periods: $1,000,000 (2/16/2021–2/15/2023); $1,022,600 (2/16/2023–2/15/2025).
- If market value exceeds FBYV + cap, the excess is added to the FBYV → partial reassessment: New assessed value = FBYV + (Market value − FBYV − cap amount).
- Non-primary-residence inheritances (rentals, vacation homes, investment property): NO exclusion → full reassessment to market value.
- Source: California State Board of Equalization, Proposition 19 page (incl. "Intergenerational Transfer Exclusion $1 Million Amount Adjustments" table and Filing Requirements Checklist): https://boe.ca.gov/prop19/

## Example: Cupertino home inherited ~2045 (projection — assumptions flagged)
- Projected factored base year value at inheritance (2026 AV $550,537 growing at the Prop 13 cap of 2%/yr for 19 years): ≈ $802,030 (modeled — assumption).
- Projected market value at inheritance (2026 estimate $3,100,000 growing 3%/yr for 19 years — assumption, not official): ≈ $5,435,870.
- If heir lives in as primary residence and files timely: new assessed value = $802,030 + ($5,435,870 − $802,030 − $1,044,586) = $4,391,284 ≈ $4,391,280 (modeled, holds the 2025-2027 cap amount constant — future cap unknown/flagged).
- If heir does NOT live in (rent/sell prep): full reassessment to ≈ $5,435,870.
- All post-inheritance figures above rely on unverified appreciation assumptions and an unknowable future cap amount — flagged for review.
