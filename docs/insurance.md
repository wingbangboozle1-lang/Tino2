# Homeowners / Rental Insurance — Research Record

## Verified benchmarks (California Department of Insurance — official)
- California does not publish one official "average cost." Two government-published series exist, measured differently and in different years; they must not be combined into one trend.
- NAIC Homeowners Report (2023 data, CA figures supplied by CDI): California average HO-3 (standard form) premium = $1,655/year (countrywide $1,737).
- CDI 2025 data call (all homeowners forms, 6,052,334 policies): statewide homeowners-market average written premium = $2,339.99/year.
- Source: California Department of Insurance data call summary / NAIC report (aggregator summary citing CDI): https://www.insurance.ca.gov (CDI home insurance data calls); https://coverageclassroom.com/learn/california-homeowners-insurance-cost (summary citing CDI/NAIC)

## ZIP-level / city-level figures (NOT official — illustrative quotes only, flagged)
No official government source publishes ZIP-code-level premiums. The figures below come from commercial insurance quoting sites; treat as indicative estimates, unverified.
- Cupertino / 95014 homeowners (primary residence): average ≈ $926–$1,189/year depending on source and coverage (gethomeinsurancequotes.com); typical insured amount for a Cupertino home ≈ $1,372,800 with average premium ≈ $1,229 (baysano.com/gavop.com). User's planning figure: $1,500/year (owner guess — unverified).
- San Francisco homeowners: average ≈ $1,154–$1,244/year; 94122 ≈ $1,235–$1,279 (policygenius.com; insuranceopedia.com; vhomeinsurance.com).
- San Francisco landlord (rental dwelling) policy: estimated ≈ $2,235/year (insurance8020.com) — representative estimate, not a quote, unverified.
- Earthquake and flood coverage are separate and not included in these figures (SF Bay Area earthquake add-on illustrative ≈ $900–$1,500/year — unverified).
