## 5. Recommendation

**Sell the Cupertino house shortly after inheritance (modeled 2045) and reinvest the ≈ $5,662,110 net proceeds. Keep the San Francisco home as primary residence plus rental.**

Reasons:
1. **Property tax dominates.** Even with Prop 19 relief, occupying the Cupertino house costs ≈ $61,440/yr in property tax alone; renting it out triggers full reassessment (≈ $74,500/yr) and yields only ≈ $26,120/yr net.
2. **Selling is nearly tax-free.** Step-up in basis (IRC § 1014) resets the cost basis to date-of-death value; selling promptly produces ≈ $0 taxable gain.
3. **Invested proceeds outperform holding.** ≈ $5,662,110 invested at a 6% planning assumption generates ≈ $339,730 in year 1, rising to ≈ $608,400/yr by 2055 — several times the net rental cash flow, with no property tax, insurance, or maintenance burden.
4. **The SF property is already a strong hold:** primary residence with ≈ $150,480/yr gross rent against a low assessed-value tax bill.
5. **Estate tax:** no federal estate tax if the total estate is under $15,000,000 per person ($30,000,000 couple, 2026); California has no estate or inheritance tax. Projected combined house values at 2045 ≈ $9,116,420 — below the exclusion unless the inherited "considerable amount of money" ([DOLLAR AMOUNT] placeholder) pushes the estate over the threshold.
