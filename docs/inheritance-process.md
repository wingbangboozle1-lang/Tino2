# Step-by-Step: Inheriting a House Held in Trust (California)

## Overview
Inheritance expected via trust around 2040–2050. A revocable living trust generally avoids probate for trust-held assets, but several legal and tax steps still apply. All steps below are the general California process; specific requirements depend on the trust document and county. ## Step-by-step process

| Step | Action | Details | Timing | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Obtain certified death certificates | Needed for trust administration, claims, tax filings, recorder documents. | Immediately after death | General practice — CA Dept of Public Health (cdph.ca.gov) |
| 2 | Locate and review the trust | Confirm trustee/successor trustee, beneficiaries, and how the houses and money are distributed. | ASAP | Trust document (private) |
| 3 | Notify beneficiaries | California Probate Code requires notice to beneficiaries and heirs (e.g., Prob. Code §16061.7 notice). | Within 120 days of trust becoming irrevocable (trustee duty) | CA Probate Code — https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov |
| 4 | Take control of trust assets | Successor trustee marshals assets: houses, bank accounts, investments; obtains tax ID for trust if needed. | After death | Trust document / IRS (EIN) |
| 5 | Get a date-of-death appraisal | Establishes stepped-up basis (IRC §1014) for each property; critical documentation for any later sale. | Before selling / for tax records | Appraiser (private); IRS Pub. 551/559 |
| 6 | File property tax exclusion claim (if applicable) | If an heir keeps the Cupertino home as a primary residence: file BOE-19-P with the county assessor within 3 years of death/transfer (before any sale to a third party, whichever is earlier); claim homeowner's exemption within 1 year to keep Prop 19 benefits. | Within 1 year (exemption) / 3 years (BOE-19-P) | CA BOE https://boe.ca.gov/prop19/ |
| 7 | Transfer title | Trustee's deed (or affidavit of death of trustee) recorded with the county recorder (Santa Clara County for Cupertino; San Francisco for SF property). | After trust administration underway | County Recorder — clerkrecorder.santaclaracounty.gov / SF Assessor-Recorder |
| 8 | File federal estate tax return if required | Form 706 due 9 months after death (6-month extension via Form 4768) only if estate exceeds the basic exclusion ($15,000,000 in 2026; indexed after). Elect portability (DSUE) for a surviving spouse if useful. | 9 months after death | IRS https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i706 |
| 9 | Handle California tax | No CA estate/inheritance tax. Income after inheritance (rent, sale gains) is taxable federally and in CA. If selling CA real estate: escrow withholds 3.33% (Form 593) unless an exemption is filed. | At sale / annually | CA FTB https://www.ftb.ca.gov |
| 10 | Sell, rent, or occupy | Execute the decision (see decision-analysis.md). Document sale price vs. stepped-up basis for capital gains reporting. | After title transfer | Owner decision |
| 11 | Ongoing compliance | Property taxes, insurance, water/electric, maintenance, rent collection and reporting if rented. | Ongoing | County tax collector; utilities; insurance carrier |

## Notes
- Assets held OUTSIDE the trust may require California probate (court-supervised), which is slower and more expensive. Confirm all property titles are in the trust's name.
- If multiple children inherit and only one lives in the Cupertino home, unequal ownership can affect the Prop 19 exclusion — consult the trustee and a CA estate attorney.
- Prop 19 requires the child to occupy as primary residence within ONE year; missing it means full reassessment to market value.
- Step-up in basis eliminates pre-death appreciation from capital gains tax but NOT post-inheritance appreciation.
- California taxes capital gains as ordinary income (top rate up to 13.3%) on top of federal 0/15/20% plus possible 3.8% net investment income tax.
