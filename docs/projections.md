## F. Recommendation

**Sell the Cupertino house shortly after inheritance (≈ 2045), reinvest the ≈ $5,662,110 net proceeds, and keep the San Francisco home as primary residence plus rental.**

Why:
1. **Property tax is the dominant cost.** Even with Prop 19 partial relief (occupying as primary residence), the Cupertino house reassesses to ≈ $4,915,530 → ≈ $61,440/yr property tax at 2045 values; without Prop 19 relief (rent it or don't move in), full reassessment → ≈ $74,500/yr. Adding insurance, water, and maintenance, *living* there costs ≈ $110,450/yr; *renting* it yields only ≈ $26,120/yr net before income tax on a $5,960,120 asset (≈ 0.4% yield).
2. **Step-up in basis makes selling nearly tax-free.** Inherited basis = date-of-death FMV (IRC § 1014). Selling promptly after inheritance produces ≈ $0 taxable gain, versus decades of untaxed appreciation that would be embedded if the parents had sold while alive.
3. **Invested proceeds dwarf holding returns.** ≈ $5,662,110 invested at a modest 6%/yr throws off ≈ $339,730 in year 1, growing to ≈ $608,400/yr by 2055 — roughly 3–5× the net cash flow from renting the Cupertino house, with no property tax, insurance, or maintenance burden.
4. **SF property is a keeper.** It already generates ≈ $150,480/yr gross rent (2 units) while the owners live there, with property tax ≈ $2,440/yr today. Projected net at 2045 ≈ $102,500/yr before income tax. Retain.
5. **Estate tax:** Federal exclusion is $15,000,000/person (2026, $30M couple with portability), indexed after 2026; California has no estate/inheritance tax. Projected combined house values at 2045 ≈ $9,116,420 — below the exclusion unless the "considerable amount of money" (unspecified, placeholder) pushes the estate over $15M/person. Above that, the estate pays 40% on the excess before distribution (paid by the estate, not heirs).

**Contingencies to monitor:** Prop 19 cap amount at time of transfer (adjusts every 2 years via BOE); whether the Cupertino solar system is on legacy NEM 1.0/2.0 (export credits) or NEM 3.0 Net Billing Tariff (exports ≈ 3.9¢/kWh — minimal profit); any federal/CA changes to estate tax or §121 exclusion; actual SF water/electric bills vs. user-provided estimates.

## G. Step-by-step: how the trust inheritance process works (California)

| # | Step | What happens | Key source |
|---|------|--------------|------------|
| 1 | Settlor (trust creator) dies | Successor trustee assumes administration; trust-held assets generally avoid probate | CA Probate Code (general trust administration) |
| 2 | Death certificates | Certified copies needed for title transfers, tax filings, insurance | Practical requirement |
| 3 | Beneficiary notice | Trustee notifies beneficiaries (e.g., within 120 days under Cal. Prob. Code § 16061.7) | Secondary summary of Prob. Code § 16061.7 |
| 4 | Identify what passes to whom | Review trust: which properties, which "considerable money", to which heirs ([HEIR NAME] placeholders) | Trust document (private) |
| 5 | File PCOR | Preliminary Change of Ownership Report filed with county assessor (Santa Clara County Assessor; SF Assessor-Recorder) on title transfer | County assessor practice |
| 6 | Record deed | Trustee's deed recorded with county recorder to transfer title | County recorder practice |
| 7 | Claim reassessment exclusion (if moving in) | File BOE-19-P with county assessor within 3 years of transfer or before sale; claim homeowner's exemption within 1 year | CA BOE Proposition 19 page (verified) |
| 8 | If not moving in | Full reassessment to market value; supplemental tax bill for the difference | CA BOE / county assessor practice |
| 9 | Document stepped-up basis | Date-of-death appraisal for real estate (basis = FMV at death, IRC § 1014) | IRS Publication 551 / IRC § 1014 (verified) |
| 10 | Estate tax return (if applicable) | IRS Form 706 due 9 months after death if gross estate > $15M (2026); portability election for married couples | IRS (verified) |
| 11 | California death taxes | None — no CA estate or inheritance tax return | CA FTB (verified) |
| 12 | Distribute money | "Considerable amount of money" distributed per trust terms ([DOLLAR AMOUNT] placeholder) | Trust document (private) |
| 13 | Ongoing ownership | Pay property tax under new assessment; maintain insurance; if selling later, § 121 exclusion ($250k/$500k) available only after owning & occupying 2 of 5 years before sale | IRS Publications 523/527 (verified) |
