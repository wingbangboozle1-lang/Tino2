A persistent $10–$20/month net credit (as user reports) is only plausible under a legacy NEM 1.0/2.0 arrangement or with battery storage; under NBT new interconnections, exports earn minimal credit. **Flagged:** verify which NEM tariff the Cupertino solar system operates under and whether legacy status transfers with the home at inheritance.
