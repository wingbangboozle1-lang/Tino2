## 3. Proposition 19 — parent-child transfer (inheritance) rules

Effective for transfers on or after February 16, 2021 (Cal. Rev. & Tax. Code § 63.2; CA State Board of Equalization).

| Rule | Detail | Source | Verified |
|------|--------|--------|----------|
| Qualifying property | Parent's principal residence only; rental/investment/second homes fully reassessed to market value | CA BOE, Proposition 19 page (boe.ca.gov/prop19) | Y |
| Child occupancy requirement | Child must make the home their principal residence within 1 year of transfer | CA BOE Proposition 19 page | Y |
| Filing | File homeowner's exemption within 1 year; file Claim for Reassessment Exclusion (Form BOE-19-P) within 3 years of transfer or before transfer to a third party, whichever is first | CA BOE Proposition 19 page | Y |
| Value cap | Exclusion limited to parent's factored base year value + $1,000,000, adjusted for inflation every 2 years by the BOE. For transfers Feb 16, 2025 – Feb 15, 2027: allowance is $1,044,586 (BOE Letter to Assessors 2025/009, cited in multiple secondary analyses) | CA BOE Proposition 19 page; secondary sources citing BOE Letter to Assessors 2025/009 | Partial (base rule official; adjusted dollar figure from secondary sources) |
| If cap exceeded | Excess over (FBYV + cap) is reassessed to market value; new assessed value = FBYV + excess | CA BOE Proposition 19 page | Y |
| If child moves out | Exclusion removed as of the January 1 lien date after move-out | Secondary analyses of RTC § 63.2 | N (secondary) |
