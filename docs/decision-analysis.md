## Key verified rules shaping the decision
1. Federal estate tax: $0 if estate ≤ $15,000,000 per person ($30,000,000 married couple w/ portability) in 2026; 40% above. Indexed for inflation after 2026. California: no estate/inheritance tax. (IRS Form 706 instructions; CA FTB — see docs/federal-estate-tax.md, california-tax.md)
2. Step-up in basis (IRC §1014): inherited property basis = fair market value at date of death. Selling soon after inheritance → little/no capital gains tax. (IRS Pub. 551/559 — verified rule)
3. Prop 19 (CA BOE): child keeps low assessed value ONLY if the child moves in as primary residence within 1 year and files BOE-19-P; cap = FBYV + $1,044,586 (current indexed amount). Otherwise full reassessment to market value. (boe.ca.gov/prop19 — verified)
4. Rental income is taxable (federal + CA); expenses (tax, insurance, water, maintenance) deductible against rents.

## Working conclusion (flagged — revisit at inheritance with verified figures)
1. KEEP the San Francisco house as a rental (very low Prop 13 tax, positive cash flow ≈ $77,100/yr modeled before maintenance). Owners can live there as primary residence and still collect rent on the other units.
2. For the Cupertino house: SELLING at inheritance and reinvesting the proceeds is the modeled best plan IF no heir needs a primary residence there, because (a) step-up basis minimizes capital gains, (b) keeping it (live-in or rental) triggers a Prop 19 reassessment with modeled annual taxes of $57,090–$70,670 — a very large carrying cost, and (c) renting it does not clearly cover that tax (break-even ≈ $5,890/mo rent before other costs).
3. If an heir wants to live in the Cupertino house, moving in within 1 year and filing BOE-19-P preserves the partial exclusion and reduces the tax hit vs. full reassessment.
4. Federal estate tax is likely $0 if total estate ≤ $15,000,000 per person ($30,000,000 couple w/ portability). If the "considerable money" plus home values exceeds that, amounts above are taxed at 40% — plan with the trustee/estate attorney.
