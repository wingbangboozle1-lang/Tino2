Step-by-step: inheriting the house through a trust

A revocable living trust generally avoids probate for trust-held assets, but legal and tax steps still apply. Timing assumes inheritance around 2040–2050. Step Action Details Timing Official source
1 Obtain certified death certificates Needed for trust administration, insurance claims, tax filings, and recorder documents. Order multiple certified copies. Immediately after death CA Dept of Public Health: cdph.ca.gov GENERAL PRACTICE
2 Locate and review the trust Confirm the [TRUSTEE]/[SUCCESSOR_TRUSTEE], beneficiaries, and how the houses and [INHERITANCE_ASSETS] are distributed. ASAP after death Trust document (private) — no public source
3 Notify beneficiaries and heirs California Probate Code requires the trustee to notify beneficiaries and heirs when the trust becomes irrevocable (e.g., Prob. Code §16061.7 notice). Within 120 days (trustee duty) CA Probate Code: leginfo.legislature.ca.gov VERIFIED
4 Take control of trust assets Successor trustee marshals the houses, bank accounts, and investments; obtains a federal tax ID (EIN) for the trust if needed. After death IRS (EIN): irs.gov VERIFIED
5 Get a date-of-death appraisal Establishes the stepped-up basis (IRC §1014) for each property — critical documentation to minimize capital gains tax on any later sale. Before any sale / for tax records IRS Pub. 551/559: irs.gov VERIFIED
6 File Proposition 19 claim (if keeping Cupertino home) To keep a reduced assessment, the child must move in as primary residence within 1 year, claim the homeowner's exemption, and file BOE-19-P with the county assessor within 3 years of death/transfer (or before sale to a third party, whichever is earlier). 1 yr (exemption) / 3 yrs (BOE-19-P) CA Board of Equalization: boe.ca.gov/prop19/ VERIFIED
7 Transfer title Trustee's deed (or affidavit of death of trustee) recorded with the county recorder — Santa Clara County (Cupertino) or San Francisco (SF property). During trust administration Santa Clara Clerk-Recorder: clerkrecorder.santaclaracounty.gov VERIFIED
8 File federal estate tax return (if required) Form 706 due 9 months after death (6-month extension via Form 4768), only if the estate exceeds the basic exclusion ($15,000,000 per person in 2026; indexed after 2026). Elect portability (DSUE) for a surviving spouse if useful. 9 months after death IRS Form 706 instructions: irs.gov/instructions/i706 VERIFIED
9 Handle California taxes No CA estate or inheritance tax. Rent and sale gains after inheritance are taxable federally and in CA (gains taxed as ordinary income, up to 13.3%). Selling CA real estate: escrow withholds 3.33% (Form 593) unless an exemption is filed. At sale / annually CA Franchise Tax Board: ftb.ca.gov VERIFIED
10 Sell, rent, or occupy Execute the decision (see Decision Analysis page). Document sale price vs. stepped-up basis for capital gains reporting. After title transfer Owner decision — no public source
11 Ongoing compliance Property taxes, insurance, water/electricity, maintenance, rent collection and income reporting if rented. Ongoing County tax collector; utility; insurer — no single public source
Important caveats. Assets held outside the trust may require California probate (court-supervised, slower and costlier) — confirm both property titles are in the trust's name. If multiple children inherit and only one lives in the Cupertino home, ownership structure affects the Proposition 19 exclusion — consult the trustee and a CA estate attorney. Proposition 19's one-year occupancy requirement is strict: missing it means full reassessment to market value.
