What happens when a trust owner dies?

Trust administration, property-tax reassessment and estate/income tax are separate processes. This is a general checklist for California trust-held real estate; the actual trust instrument, ownership and then-current law control.

Time-sensitive legal checklist—not individual legal advice

The county assessor filing and Prop 19 claim deadlines are separate. Do not rely on this summary to calculate a deadline; have the successor trustee and California counsel confirm trigger dates, forms, and any exception. A trust does not by itself guarantee a property-tax exclusion or eliminate federal estate tax.

Trust-property process, in order

Confirm successor authority

Locate the trust and amendments, confirm the successor trustee accepts the role, obtain certified death certificates, and establish who has present beneficial ownership. A trust can avoid court probate for properly trust-owned property, but terms and asset title matter. California Probate Code §16000 ↗

Give required notices

If a revocable trust (or portion) becomes irrevocable because of death, California Probate Code §16061.7 requires trustee notification to specified beneficiaries/heirs within the statutory 60-day period. The trigger and service details depend on the facts; follow the statute and trust. California Probate Code §16061.7 ↗

Notify the county assessor

When an interest in California real property transfers by reason of death through a trust, the trustee or transferee generally must file a change-in-ownership statement with the county recorder or assessor within 150 days after death. Probate cases follow the statute’s separate timing. Confirm the applicable form and deadline with counsel and the assessor. California Revenue & Taxation Code §480 ↗

Secure and inventory assets

Protect the home, notify insurer and utilities, document condition and leases, identify debts and liens, and prepare a date-of-death inventory/appraisal. Inventory is also needed to assess estate-tax filing and inherited income-tax basis. IRS Form 706 valuation and filing guidance ↗

Review federal returns and elections

Determine whether Form 706 is due, account for adjusted taxable gifts and deductions, and evaluate a portability election for a surviving spouse. The IRS states Form 706 generally is due nine months after death; an extension may be available. IRS Form 706 instructions ↗

Apply for the Prop 19 exclusion, if eligible

For a qualifying parent-to-child (or limited grandparent-to-grandchild) family-home transfer, transferee must make it their principal residence and claim the homeowners’ or disabled-veteran exemption within one year. File the exclusion claim promptly; the BOE fact sheet discusses the statutory three-year/third-party-transfer timing. BOE Proposition 19 fact sheet ↗

Obtain the assessor’s enrolled value and tax bill

Ask how the transfer is classified, whether any exclusion applies, whether supplemental assessment will issue, and what local bonds/direct assessments apply. A county-assessed value is not the same thing as market value or a tax bill. California BOE assessment overview ↗

Choose operate, occupy, distribute or sell

Follow trust distribution terms, settle allowed debts/expenses, report trust and rental income, and keep records of basis, improvements, depreciation and sale costs. Get legal and tax advice before distributing, refinancing or selling. IRS rental tax guide ↗

Federal inheritance-tax threshold: what “over the limit” means

Estate tax is calculated on the decedent’s estate

For 2026, the federal basic exclusion is $15,000,000, indexed for inflation for years beginning after 2026 under current enacted law. A federal filing requirement generally applies when gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts exceeds the threshold; the return can also be required to elect portability. Inclusion, deductions and valuation rules are fact-specific.

IRS Instructions for Form 706 ↗

Do not project a 2040–2050 threshold as a fact

Future indexed amounts are not published yet. The IRS states the 2026 threshold and the statutory inflation-adjustment mechanism; an assumed CPI path would be a scenario, not an official threshold. The dashboard therefore leaves future thresholds blank.
