Privacy treatment

User-submitted property characteristics, location identifiers, household utility details, rent figures, assessments, valuations and other personal financial information are excluded from this public site. They were not independently validated against assessor records, bills, leases, appraisals, insurance policies or trust documents. Do not treat them as confirmed inputs.

Law-change status

Verified as enacted for the 2026 baseline

The IRS lists a $15,000,000 basic exclusion amount for 2026 under Public Law 119-21, with inflation adjustments for years after 2026. It is the enacted baseline—not a guaranteed 2040–2050 amount. This guide does not label an unenacted bill as current law.
