Reusable formulas—not forecasts

Property market-value scenario

Year t value = independently appraised base value × (1 + selected annual growth assumption)^(t − base year)

Choose low/base/high assumptions and label their source and date. A generic inflation measure is not a forecast for a specific California property.

BLS CPI data (inflation history, not home-price forecast) ↗

Assessed value / annual property tax

Taxable value = county-assessed factored base-year value, subject to market-value reductions and applicable exclusions.
Tax bill = taxable value × parcel tax rate + direct assessments.

Use the county notice and bill. Prop 13’s maximum annual factored growth is generally the inflation factor, capped at 2%; a transfer may reset the base year unless an exclusion applies.

BOE Publication 29 ↗

Rental net operating cash flow

Collected rent − vacancy/credit loss − operating expenses − capital reserve − debt service − income taxes

Keep operating costs separate from depreciation and financing. Taxable rental income can differ from cash flow. Allocate mixed personal and rental use under current tax rules.

IRS Publication 527 ↗

Sell and reinvest

Net investable proceeds = sale price − selling costs − loan payoff − tax on taxable gain

Compare against a diversified allocation and include fees, taxes, inflation, liquidity needs and downside scenarios. No return is guaranteed; securities can lose principal.
