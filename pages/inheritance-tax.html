3. What this means for the estate

  • No federal estate tax if the total estate (both houses + inherited money) is at or below $15,000,000 per person ($30,000,000 per married couple with portability).
  • Projected combined house values at 2045 (model): ≈ $9,116,420 — below the exclusion unless the inherited money ([DOLLAR AMOUNT]) pushes the estate over $15,000,000 per person.
  • If above the threshold: the estate pays 40% on the excess before any distribution; California adds nothing.
  • Inherited assets (cash, securities, real estate) receive a step-up in basis to date-of-death value (see Basis & Capital Gains page).

4. Law changes under discussion

  • No enacted federal or California change to estate/inheritance thresholds as of Nov 2026 beyond P.L. 119-21 (already reflected in the $15,000,000 figure).
  • Some secondary sources describe the $15,000,000 level as permanent; others reference possible later changes. The IRS page (authoritative) confirms $15,000,000 for 2026 and inflation indexing from 2027. Future Congresses can change the law — re-verify near the actual inheritance date.
  • Proposals such as the "For the 99.5 Percent Act" (lowered exemption, graduated rates) were not enacted.
