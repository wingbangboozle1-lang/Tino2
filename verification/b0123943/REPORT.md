## 5. What would change the headline answer (carried from the published Best plan, independently consistent with this report's checks)

1. **Cupertino prices grow ≳7.8%/yr after inheritance** (vs 3.5% assumed) → renting overtakes selling. County prices rose ≈7.0%/yr 1975–2025 but only ≈4.3%/yr 2000–2025, with a 25% fall 2006–2011 (FHFA via FRED; series re-verified in the verification ledger). [FHFA — ATNHPIUS06085A](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/ATNHPIUS06085A)
2. **After-tax portfolio return falls below ≈2.8%/yr** → renting overtakes selling at the base 3.5% home growth. (Deposits are FDIC-insured only to $250,000 per depositor, per bank, per ownership category. [FDIC](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance/understanding-deposit-insurance))
3. **The heir wants to live in Property A** → choose Plan 3 or 4; move in within one year and file the homeowners' exemption within one year plus the Prop 19 exclusion claim within three years (BOE LTA 2026/026; Prob. Code §16061.7; RTC §480 deadlines in §2 above). [BOE LTA 2026/026](https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta26026.pdf)
4. **Step-up basis is repealed/limited before the inheritance** → the sell case's tax advantage narrows; every plan is hit when the house is finally sold.
5. **Proposition 13/19 changes or SF File No. 261020-type caps are enacted** → shifts rental economics; November 2026 ballot measures (Props 40/42/43) did not alter the 1% levy or 2% cap (unofficial counts, certification due 2026-12-11). [CA Secretary of State — 2026 ballot measures](https://electionresults.sos.ca.gov/returns/ballot-measures)
6. **More than one child inherits** → only if the trust allows uneven distribution and the home does not exceed that child's share can reassessment be avoided (BOE LTA 2026/026 Q41–Q42). [BOE LTA 2026/026](https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta26026.pdf)

**Base-case headline (assumptions on the site, rounded to $10):** for a 2045 inheritance, selling Property A at inheritance finishes ≈ **$9,125,340** at 2055 ($4,719,060 in 2026 dollars), ahead of renting by ≈ $1,729,720 and ahead of living there by ≈ $3,057,260; selling wins in 10 of 12 recorded-episode scenarios and in 76% of 2,000 block-bootstrap 50-year histories (62% when the portfolio's end growth is also taxed).
